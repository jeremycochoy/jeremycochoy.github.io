Methodology note — Household and business prices are not directly comparable. Household prices include VAT (ranging from 5% in Malta to 25% in several countries) and all levies. Business prices exclude recoverable VAT but include non-recoverable taxes. In countries like Hungary, Malta and Bulgaria, household prices are heavily regulated or capped by government, making them artificially low compared to market rates that businesses pay. Conversely, in Germany and Denmark, heavy energy taxes and renewable levies inflate household bills by 40–50%, while businesses are partially exempt.
